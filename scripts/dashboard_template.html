Recursive VAR — a 4‑variable VAR (level, slope, curvature of the yield curve), re‑estimated at every date using only data available through that date, used to project the average expected short rate over the next 10 years. Term premium = 10‑year yield minus that projection.
Discounted VAR — the same method, estimated by weighted least squares with geometrically decaying weights (ρ=0.99722), demeaned with the same weights so the reversion target stays consistent with the discounted dynamics; ρ is fixed so the total weight mass matches a 30‑year window (N_eff = 1/(1−ρ) = 360 months).
Kim‑Wright — term‑premium model published by the Federal Reserve Board.
ACM — term‑premium model (Adrian‑Crump‑Moench) published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York.

Recursive / Discounted long-run level — the level each method’s VAR demeans the short rate toward: a plain historical average for Recursive, a ρ=0.99722‑weighted average for Discounted (the same weighting used in its regression).
SEP long-run federal funds rate — median longer‑run projection from the FOMC’s Summary of Economic Projections (FRED series FEDTARMDLR), updated quarterly and held flat between releases.
